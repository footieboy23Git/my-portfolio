## Impact Highlights
* **Zero data loss** preserved operational and overtime tracking.
* **Global tracking functionality restored** during a live production period without external support.

## Situation
During a peak production period, a critical failure disrupted the global time-tracking infrastructure used across Artworking, Photography, Account Management, and Image Editing. The failure prevented teams from recording live capacity and working hours, creating risk to operational reporting and overtime tracking.

## Action
* **Independent Investigation:** Took ownership of the outage during a weekend, investigating automation behavior to isolate the failure to a third-party script.
* **Native Rebuild:** Rebuilt the required automation logic using native monday.com functionality.
* **Testing & Restoration:** Thoroughly tested the replacement workflow before restoring system access across affected global teams.
* **Architecture Governance:** Conducted a post-incident review and recommended moving critical features away from unnecessary third-party dependencies.

## Result
Restored global time tracking during a critical production period, preserving underlying tracking data and establishing a more resilient system architecture.
