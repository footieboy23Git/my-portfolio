## Impact Highlights
* **Direct management** of a dedicated 3-person creative operations team.
* **Seamless operational delivery** across briefing, quality control, and resource allocation.

## Situation
Managing a high-volume studio environment required continuous balance between incoming brief volume, designer allocation, tight delivery deadlines, and maintaining top-tier creative output quality.

## Action
* **End-to-End Oversight:** Oversaw the daily operational flow from initial client briefing through brief assessment and final output.
* **Resource Allocation:** Strategically balanced workloads across internal designers and external support to maintain output without burnout.
* **Quality & Governance:** Maintained strict quality control processes and production standards on all outgoing campaign assets.
* **Team Leadership:** Managed, mentored, and supported a team of 3, fostering a collaborative and efficient studio environment.

## Result
Established a structured, predictable studio environment capable of consistently meeting tight project deadlines while maintaining high creative and operational standards.
